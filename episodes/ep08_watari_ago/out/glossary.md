# Glossary: Episode 8, Beam Over Beam: Watari-ago

The model follows a Japanese dictionary's description (精選版 日本国語大辞典, with JAANUS). Its depths are illustrative: no modern specification gives one (R8-23). Kanji only for verified terms; spec spellings where the episode cites the spec.

| Romaji | Kanji | Kana | English gloss | One-line description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| watari-ago | 渡り腮 / 渡りあご | わたりあご | crossing joint | A shiguchi: a groove across the upper timber's underside; the lower timber cut away left and right, its middle left standing. (R8-01, R8-11, R8-33) |
| ago | 腮 | あご | jaw | Here read ago; on its own also read えら era, "gills". Which part is the jaw: not stated in our sources. (R8-02, R8-15) |
| watari | 渡り | わたり | a crossing | Everyday sense, from the dictionary; not offered as the joint's etymology. (R8-32) |
| shiguchi | 仕口 | しぐち | joint at an angle | Also read shikuchi. Its counterpart, tsugite 継手, is a splice. (R8-35, R8-41) |
| aigaki | 相欠き | あいがき | half-lap | Each timber cut to half its depth; tops flush. Not the same joint. (R8-04, R8-13) |
| kabuto-arikake | かぶとあり掛け | かぶとありかけ | a dovetail | Flat 35's other choice where a roof beam meets the eaves plate. (R8-07, R8-16) |
| (housed dovetail) | 大入れあり掛け | — | housed dovetail | Flat 35's choice where the tops are to finish flush; not a crossing joint. (R8-43) |
| (plate bolt) | 羽子板ボルト | (reading not verified) | plate bolt | A 3.2 mm steel plate with a 12 mm bolt welded to it. (R8-34, R8-38) |
