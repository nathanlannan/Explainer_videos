# Glossary: Episode 9, The Gooseneck: Koshikake Kama-tsugi

Sizes in the episode are one test series' specimen geometry on 120 mm timbers (denmoku-db, Toda 2012; R9-46). No carpentry source gives standard sizes (R9-47). Kanji only for verified terms.

| Romaji | Kanji | Kana | English gloss | One-line description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| koshikake kama-tsugi | 腰掛鎌継ぎ | こしかけかまつぎ | seated gooseneck splice | A head on a neck in the upper half, a seat in the lower half; the male (upper piece) drops in from above. (R9-04, R9-16, R9-17) |
| kama-tsugi | 鎌継ぎ | かまつぎ | gooseneck splice | Read かまつぎ in two dictionaries; also shortened to 鎌 kama. "Gooseneck" is an English name. (R9-01, R9-02, R9-06) |
| kama | 鎌 | かま | head (of the tenon) | The head, per JAANUS's and the training course's labels; on its own, a sickle. (R9-08, R9-13) |
| kubi | 首 | くび | neck | Between the shoulder and the head. (R9-13) |
| kamakubi | 鎌首 | かまくび | a snake's raised head and neck | Literally a neck curved like a sickle. Two sources compare the tenon to one; no etymology is claimed. (R9-07, R9-56, R9-57) |
| koshikake | 腰掛 | こしかけ | seat | The female's lower-half ledge the male rests on. (R9-13, R9-16) |
| ogi / megi | 男木 / 女木 | おぎ / めぎ | male / female | The upper piece / the lower piece. (R9-14) |
| (stub tenon) | 目違い | — | stub tenon | Resists twisting; added no pulling strength in one test series (T). (R9-22, R9-23) |
