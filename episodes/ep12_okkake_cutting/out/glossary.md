# Glossary: Episode 12, Cutting the Okkake

The sequence follows one training course (ものつくり大学 WBT, 第6日目, t0811–t0826; R11-53, R11-54). Others may order things differently. The model is Episode 11's (one lab's test proportions); every cut state is an exact solid, and the last equals Episode 11's lower piece. Kanji only for verified terms.
