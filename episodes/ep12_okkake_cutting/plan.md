Model: the Ep11 lower piece (W 120, H 180, splice 360; Toda's test proportions) in cut states: L0 blank (+25 mm end slice) → L1 peg holes started (W/4 deep from the outer face) → L2 zone A outer block removed (x from just before the slope to the tip, z > e) → L3 zone B outer layer removed (x 0 … L/2, z > e) → L4 center strip near the shoulder removed to the slope (x 0 … xs(y), |z| < e) → L5 housing + tongue + holes = the Ep11 lower piece.

Cut states (Gate A, exact; Ep11 frame, e = 7.5, F = 15, L = 360, xs(y) = L/2 + (y − H/2)/20):
- L0 blank to x = L + F + 25; the end cut takes the 25 mm slice; end-grain lines at z = ±e.
- L1 holes 15 × 15 at x = 90 and 270, y 82.5–97.5, from z = −60 to −30.
- L2 remove {x in [L/2, L+F], z in [e, W/2]}; the rip kerf at z = +e runs to x ≈ 165 (just past the slope).
- L3 remove {x in [0, L/2], z in [e, W/2]} (shoulder crosscut at x = 0; split from the slope end).
- L4 slope line on the split face, then remove {x in [0, xs(y)], |z| ≤ e}.
- L5 housing {x in [−15, 0], |z| < e}; tongue waste {x in [L, L+F], z in [−W/2, −e]}; holes through the kept wood. Equals Ep11's lower piece.
