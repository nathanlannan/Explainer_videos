# Glossary: Episode 10, Cutting the Gooseneck

The sequence follows one training course (ものつくり大学 WBT, 第5日目 腰掛け鎌継ぎ目違い付, t0711–t0728; R9-58 to R9-61). That exercise adds a stub tenon at the seat; the episode leaves it out. Others may order things differently. Sizes are Episode 9's test-specimen geometry. Kanji only for verified terms.
