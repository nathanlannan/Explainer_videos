# Glossary: Episode 5, The First Joint: Koshikake Ari-tsugi

Kanji appear only for verified terms (see `research/ep05_fact_pack.md`, rows R6-xx).

| Romaji | Kanji | Kana | English gloss | One-line description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| koshikake ari-tsugi | 腰掛蟻継ぎ (also 腰掛けあり継ぎ) | こしかけありつぎ | seated dovetail splice | Joins two members end to end. One of two sill splices named in the Flat 35 specification. Also called shikimen ari-tsugi 敷面蟻継. (R6-01–R6-03, R6-07) |
| tsugite | 継手 | つぎて | splice | Joins members end to end, in line. (R6-04) |
| shiguchi | 仕口 | しぐち (also しくち) | angle joint | Joins members meeting at an angle. (R6-04, R6-05) |
| ari | 蟻 | あり | dovetail | The flaring shape. (R6-04) |
| koshikake | 腰掛 | こしかけ | seat | The female's lower-half ledge that the male sits on; also shikimen 敷面. (R6-03, R6-14) |
| ogi / megi | 男木 / 女木 | おぎ / めぎ | male / female | The male carries the dovetail and is the upper piece; it drops in from above. (R6-13, R6-14) |
| ōbiki | 大引 | おおびき | ground-floor beam | Carries the floor joists; also spliced this way. (R6-06) |
| — | 胴付き | どうづき | shoulder | Where the dovetail's neck meets the body. (R6-16) |
| — | — | — | placement | Away from posts and underfloor vents (Flat 35); the anchor bolt goes in the upper piece, beside the joint (public-works specification). (R6-07, R6-09, R6-15) |
| — | — | — | proportions | One engineering study's ranges: neck ¼–⅓ of the width; dovetail ¼–½ of the width long. The episode's drawing sits inside both, with sides sloping about 1 in 5. (R6-19, R6-22) |
| — | — | — | limits | Weak in tension; almost a hinge in bending (one test report); fails suddenly. Keep knots out. (R6-11, R6-12, R6-32) |
