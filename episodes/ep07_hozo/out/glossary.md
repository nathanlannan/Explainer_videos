# Glossary: Episode 7, Mortise and Tenon: Hozo

Sizes on screen carry their sources: the 105 mm short tenon comes from a hardware test standard (howtec, R7-19); the long tenon from lab specimens on 120 mm members (Toda 2015, R7-20). No sourced long-tenon size exists for 105 mm posts (R7-21). Kanji only for verified terms.

| Romaji | Kanji | Kana | English gloss | One-line description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| hozo | 枘 | ほぞ | tenon | The tongue on a member's end. Older reading ほそ hoso. (R7-01, R7-36) |
| hozo-ana | 枘穴 | ほぞあな | mortise | Literally "tenon hole"; no rendaku. (R7-02) |
| jūhozo | 重ほぞ | じゅうほぞ | stepped ("stacked") tenon | At a post top where a wall plate and a beam stack. (R7-06, R7-16) |
| ōgi-hozo | 扇ほぞ | おうぎほぞ | fan tenon | Trapezoid section; used for a post at an outside sill corner, with hardware. (R7-05, R7-11, R7-12) |
| komisen | 込み栓 | こみせん | draw pin | Hardwood pin driven sideways through sill and tenon; the holes are made so it draws the members together. (R7-07, R7-31, R7-33) |
| hanasen | 鼻栓 | はなせん | pin | A pin through the projecting part of a through-tenon. (R7-08, R7-15) |
