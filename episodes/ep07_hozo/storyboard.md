# Episode 7: Mortise and Tenon, Hozo (Gate A plan)

Facts come from `research/ep07_fact_pack.md` (R7-xx). The geometry is parametric and fit-checked (`params.py`):
- **Short tenon:** 105 mm post and sill; tenon 30 × 85 × 50 mm (R7-19, the hardware test standard, attributed).
- **Long tenon + komisen:** drawn on 120 mm members using the lab-specimen sizes (R7-20, attributed; there is no sourced 105 mm size, R7-21). Tenon 30 × 90 × 117 mm; an 18 mm square pin at sill mid-height (R7-34 square holes).
- **Drawbore offset:** shown in a 2D diagram, exaggerated, with "a millimetre or two, by one account" kept off screen as a rule (R7-32 is not a standard).
