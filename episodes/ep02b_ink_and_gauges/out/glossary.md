# Glossary: Episode 2 · Part 2, Ink Line, Pen and Gauges

Kanji appear only for terms verified in independent sources (see `research/research_table.md`, rows R3-xx). Pronunciation notes are in `shared/audio/voice/pronunciation.md`.

| Romaji | Kanji | Kana | English gloss | One-line description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| sumitsubo | 墨壺 | すみつぼ | ink line | A pot with an inked pond, a reel, a line and a pin. The line is pinned, pulled taut, lifted straight up and released, and snaps a straight line even over slight unevenness. Mulberry traditionally; zelkova common today. Pots about 18, 24 or 30 cm. (R3-01–03) |
| ike | 池 | いけ | pond | The well of the pot holding floss soaked in ink; the line runs through it. (R3-01) |
| karuko | 軽子 (also 仮子) | かるこ | pin | The pin at the line's free end, pushed into the timber. (R3-01) |
| sumisashi | 墨差し | すみさし | ink pen | Flattened bamboo. One end is cut on a slant and split into fine slits for lines; the other is rounded and crushed, like a brush, for writing marks. (R3-07) |
| kebiki | 罫引 (also 毛引) | けびき | marking gauge | A fence (jōgi-ita 定規板) slides on a true edge, and a small blade at the end of the beam (sao 棹) scores a parallel line. Set once, it repeats on every piece. (R3-09) |
| sujikebiki | 筋罫引 | すじけびき | scoring gauge | Scores lines. (R3-10) |
| warikebiki | 割罫引 | わりけびき | slitting gauge | Cuts deeper, to split thin boards to width. (R3-10) |
| shiragaki / shirabiki | — (kanji vary) | しらがき / しらびき | marking knife | Most have a flat back and one bevel. It cuts a very fine line, mostly across the grain. (R3-13) |
| squaring a line | — | — | — | Hook one arm of the sashigane against a straight, true edge, press near its middle, and draw along the other arm. Which arm hooks varies. (R3-14) |
