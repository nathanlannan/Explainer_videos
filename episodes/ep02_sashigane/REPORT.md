The bench exercise is a printable 1:1 back scale; I verified its 100 mm calibration bar at 254 dpi.

**One idea:** the back scale (ura-me 裏目) is the front scale × √2, so it turns a diagonal into the side. Across a log's small end it reads the side of the largest square beam: 300 mm reads 212.
