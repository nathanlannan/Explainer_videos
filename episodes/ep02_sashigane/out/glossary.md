# Glossary: Episode 2 · Part 1, The Marking Kit: The Sashigane

Kanji appear only for terms verified in two independent sources (see `research/research_table.md`, rows R2-xx). Pronunciation notes are in `shared/audio/voice/pronunciation.md`.

| Romaji | Kanji | Kana | English gloss | One-line description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| sashigane | 差金 (also 指矩, 指金) | さしがね | carpenter's square | A thin L of steel (today usually stainless) that is both a square and a ruler. Also called 曲尺 kanejaku. Common size is scaled to 500 × 250 mm, about 15 mm wide and about 1.4 mm thick (JIS A形). (R2-01–05) |
| nagate | 長手 | ながて | long arm | The long arm of the sashigane. (R2-02) |
| tsumate | 妻手 | つまて (also つまで) | short arm | The short arm of the sashigane. (R2-02) |
| omote-me | 表目 | おもてめ | front scale | Ordinary units, usually millimeters. Some squares use traditional shaku units. (R2-08, R2-13) |
| ura-me | 裏目 | うらめ | back scale (×√2) | Each unit is √2 ≈ 1.414 front units, so a measured diagonal reads as the square's side. Across a log's small end it gives the side of the largest square beam. Formally called kakume 角目. (R2-09, R2-10) |
| marume | 丸目 | まるめ | round scale (÷π) | Each unit is 1/π of a front unit. Laid across a diameter, it reads the circumference. Only some squares have it. (R2-11) |
| kikujutsu | 規矩術 | きくじゅつ | carpenter's layout geometry | The traditional geometry of roof slopes and hips, done with the front and back scales. Named only; beyond this series. (R2-14) |
