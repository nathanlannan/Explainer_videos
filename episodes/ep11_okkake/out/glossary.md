# Glossary: Episode 11, The Other Splice: Okkake Daisen-tsugi

Model sizes are one lab's test proportions (Toda 2012, OD-1836: 120 wide, 180 deep, splice 360; R11-32). The joint line is drawn straight (the drawings show it slightly oblique). Kanji only for verified terms.

| Romaji | Kanji | Kana | English gloss | One-line description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| okkake daisen-tsugi | 追掛け大栓継ぎ | おっかけだいせんつぎ | a pegged splice | Two pieces of the same shape, turned end for end. Over the splice each keeps about half the width at full depth; the upper is lowered onto the lower; two pegs from opposite sides. JAANUS: "an oblique, housed (dadoed) and rabbeted scarf joint". (R11-01, R11-04, R11-07, R11-10, R11-52) |
| okkake-tsugi | 追掛継 | おっかけつぎ | the same, unpegged | The name without the pegs. (R11-03) |
| daisen | 大栓 | だいせん | the pegs | Written 大 "large" + 栓 "peg". Hardwood; they stop the joint lifting out. (R11-16, R11-17, R11-51) |
| ago | 顎 (also 腮) | あご | the step at the middle | Its face is sloped (1/20 of the depth in one source, about 1/10 in another); as the upper drops, the slope draws the shoulders closed. (R11-09, R11-11, R11-12) |
| mechigai | 目違 | めちがい | end tongue | A small tongue at each end, into the other piece; stops the pieces coming apart sideways. (R11-09, R11-50) |
| (lower / upper piece) | 下木 / 上木 | — | lower / upper piece | The lower is set first; the upper is lowered onto it. The slope runs the other way on the upper piece (one training course). (R11-07, R11-08) |
