Model (`params.py`): W 120 × H 180 timbers; splice L = 360; each piece half the width over the splice; ago 15 mm (7.5 either side of center) at mid-length, its face sloped 1/20 of the depth; mechigai 15 × 15 full depth at each end; pegs 15 mm square at L/4 either side of the center, mid-depth, driven from opposite sides. Checks: no interference; contact; drop-in clear; locked against pull-apart and sideways; lifts out without pegs; pegs stop the lift; the pieces congruent apart from the slope, which is reversed on the upper piece. On screen: "simplified: in the drawings the joint line runs slightly oblique". Under tension the slope pushes the upper piece up; the pegs resist it.
