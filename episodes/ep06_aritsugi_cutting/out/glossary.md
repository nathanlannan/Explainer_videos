# Glossary: Episode 6, Marking Out and Cutting the Koshikake Ari-tsugi

The sequence follows one training course (ものつくり大学 WBT, R6-26 to R6-29); others may order things differently. Kanji only for verified terms.

| Romaji | Kanji | Kana | English gloss | One-line description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| (end line) | 砂切り | (reading unconfirmed) | end crosscut line | The end is cut square at this line. Its reading is not verified, so the episode calls it "the end line". (R6-16) |
| koshikake | 腰掛 | こしかけ | seat | The female's lower-half ledge. (R6-14) |
| ari | 蟻 | あり | dovetail | Its sides angled using the width of the sashigane's blade. (R6-20) |
| split the line | — | — | sumi o matagu | The kerf centered on the line (Episode 4). The seat rip and crosscut, and the shoulder crosscuts, split the line. (R6-28, R6-29) |
| taper | — | — | cheeks | On the male's cheeks the line is left at the top and taken at the bottom, so it enters easily and tightens. (R6-24) |
| — | — | — | tip over-dig | At the socket's tip, dig about 1 bu (≈3 mm) more than the line, so the tip can't bear first. This episode reads it as deeper; the source is ambiguous. (R6-28) |
| kigoroshi | 木殺し | きごろし | if too tight | A general carpentry manual's remedy; the gennō's domed face is the kigoroshi face (Episode 4). (R6-30, R5-21) |
