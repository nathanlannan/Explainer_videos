Gathered by a research agent, 2026-10-25. Short names used below:
- **MHLW** — the national skills-test (建築大工) training manual, with page numbers.
- **K-x** — kotobank.jp/word/x, which quotes 世界大百科 (WBH), ニッポニカ (Nip) and 精選版/大辞泉.
- **JAANUS** — aisf.or.jp/~jaanus.
- **TCTM** — Takenaka Carpentry Tools Museum, dougukan.jp/tools/10.
- **Odate93** — "Choosing and Using Japanese Handsaws", *Fine Woodworking* #101.
- **Odate81** — "Japanese Blades", *Fine Woodworking* #29.
- **Fox13** — *Fine Woodworking* #233.
- **Okimoto90** — Takenaka museum bulletin, vol. 2.

**Avoid:**
- JAANUS's "dozuki about 2 mm thick"; retail plates are about 0.2–0.3 mm.
- Saying the ryōba or the chip-breaker is "ancient".
- Saying planes or saws have "always" been pulled. The block plane spread only in the 16th century; before that, the spear plane was used.
- Saying the domed gennō face is for striking chisels.
- Saying chisels are hollow-ground.
- Unsourced 墨を殺す/生かす and 半墨.
