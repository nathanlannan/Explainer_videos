**Avoid saying:**
- "The most common splice." The supported claim is that it is one of two standard sill splices in the JHF spec.
- That it is "strong in tension".
- "1:6–1:8".
- Any fixed "standard" dimensions.
- That the female goes on top, or that the male slides in sideways.
- That the MHLW 3級 manual covers this joint.
- "arizugi".
- Calling it a shiguchi.
- "Ant joint". 蟻 is literally "ant", but here it names the dovetail shape.
