# Episode 8 fact pack: 渡り腮 watari-ago and other crossing joints

Gathered by a research agent, 2026-10-25. Status: **verified** (2+ independent sources), **single-source**, **unverified**.

**Sources:**
- **F**: Flat 35 木造住宅工事仕様書 2025 (sample PDF; page numbers as printed nn/203).
- **L**: MLIT 公共建築木造工事標準仕様書 令和7年版.
- **H**: 日本住宅・木材技術センター 木造住宅用標準納まり図 (H17), p.18 and p.4.
- **KD**: kotobank 渡り腮 (デジタル大辞泉).
- **KN**: kotobank 渡腮 (精選版 日本国語大辞典).
- **J**: JAANUS: watariago, aigaki, arikake, agokaki, kyourogumi, daimochitsugi.
- **R**: 建築基準法施行令 令44条, 令47条 (e-Gov).
- **NK**: 『日本家屋構造』 (明治37), seen only through a secondary repost.

**Avoid:**
- "The jaw hooks over".
- Calling it a half-lap.
- Saying only one member is notched.
- "45 degrees".
- Saying MLIT prescribes it.
- Giving any notch depth as a standard.
- Calling 大入れ蟻掛け a crossing joint.
- Saying F names 胴差し.
- Web strength figures.
- あいかき.
- Treating 渡り欠き as a synonym.
