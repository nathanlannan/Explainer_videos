**Avoid:**
- Presenting 90×60 as the standard for 105 mm.
- Any 105 mm long-tenon or pin size.
- "Always one third of the post."
- Presenting a 2–3 mm offset as a rule, or using 引き勝手.
- "Komisen are round."
- Presenting 厚み緩め/幅きつめ as a post-to-sill rule.
- "MHLW says chop from both faces."
- "Pinned long tenon is stronger than hardware."
- Specifics about 小根ほぞ.
- The Gifu snippet claim.
- "込栓 is only for sills."
